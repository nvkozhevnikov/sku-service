   are normal; characteristics still corroborate, disambiguate and contradict.

   an exact scoped equivalence is proven. Descriptive prose plus is separate.

   or diameter above bed to above carriage. Align meaning/unit/role first.
4. Ignoring a proven same-role material power/capacity/bore disagreement or silently
   fixing inconvenient canonical values. Retain raw values/provenance; non-auto.
5. Auto-accepting fuzzy/base family/similar specs despite different full executions.
6. Turning retrieval synonyms into identity aliases, or extending a scoped rebrand
   to arbitrary models. Exact variants in series titles are not arbitrary parents.
7. Matching a jaw/controller/handle to the parent machine by its reference model.
8. Publishing old rejected704 or claiming zero SQL writes for audit-only replay.
